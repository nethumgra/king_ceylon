The King Ceylon Journey

Founded in 2020, King Ceylon was born from a simple passion: bringing the authentic taste of Sri Lankan jackfruit to the world. Our journey began in the lush farms of central Sri Lanka, where jackfruit trees have thrived for centuries.

What started as a small family operation has grown into a proud ambassador of Sri Lankan cuisine and culture, while maintaining our commitment to quality, sustainability, and ethical sourcing.

Sustainability

Our Commitment to the Planet

At King Ceylon, we believe in sustainable practices at every step of our journey, from farm to table.

Why Jackfruit Matters

Jackfruit is one of the most sustainable crops in the world. A single tree can produce up to 200 fruits per year, with each fruit weighing up to 100 pounds!

By choosing jackfruit products, you're supporting a food source that:

  • Requires minimal water and resources to grow
  • Is naturally pest-resistant, reducing the need for pesticides
  • Produces high yields with a small carbon footprint
  • Provides sustainable income for local farmers
